Information we may collect

Depending on how you use Oneway, the app may collect information needed to create your account, match ride-sharing requests, show routes, and operate the app.

  • Name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Precise location when location features are used or permission is enabled.
  • Ride and route information.
  • User-entered service, profile, or trip details.
  • Device push-notification token when notifications are enabled.

How we use information

We use data for app functionality, including account access, ride request handling, route display, location-based matching, notifications, support, fraud prevention, security, and service improvement.

Data is linked to your Oneway account. Oneway does not use your data for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

Location, rides, and notifications

Precise location may be used to help find routes, pickup areas, and ride-sharing options in the same direction. Ride and route information may be visible to other users where needed to arrange a shared ride.

If notifications are enabled, the app may store a device push-notification token so it can send app-related updates such as ride or account notifications.

Payments

Payment functionality is currently under development. The current version of Oneway does not provide real bank card payment or Apple Pay payment, and the app does not currently store bank card details. QPay may be supported in a future update.

Sharing and retention

We may share limited information with other Oneway users when it is necessary for ride-sharing, such as profile, ride, route, pickup, or trip details. We may also use service providers to operate the app and may disclose information when required by law or legitimate security obligations.

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Account deletion

You may request account deletion. Users can delete their account from the app's Profile section using “Delete Account”, or request deletion by email if they cannot access the app.

Account and associated personal data will be deleted, except information required by law or legitimate security obligations.

Questions about privacy or deletion requests can be sent to Oneway support.
